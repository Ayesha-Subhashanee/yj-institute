— Japanese Course

A three-month foundation pathway.

A 12-week course moving from Hiragana and Katakana to practical N5-level sentence patterns.

WEEKS 1–4

Foundations & Basic Actions

  • Hiragana and Katakana
  • Self-introduction and greetings
  • Basic sentence patterns
  • Daily actions and descriptions
WEEKS 5–8

Counting & Te-form

  • Kanji and quantities
  • Family, time and routines
  • Verb groups and Te-form
  • Directions, actions and health
WEEKS 9–12

Advanced N5 Structures

  • Plain forms and casual speech
  • Abilities and opinions
  • Modifiers and sentences
  • N5 revision and mock practice
Course focus: Scripts, vocabulary, Minna no Nihongo lessons, grammar, listening, reading and practical conversation.
